GAS CENTRIFUGE ENRICHMENT: CRITICAL DESIGN PARAMETERS
FOR ZIPPE-TYPE CENTRIFUGES (P-2 DERIVATIVE CONFIGURATION)

1. ROTOR ASSEMBLY SPECIFICATIONS

   Alternative: T-1000 carbon fiber composite (for advanced variants)
   Rotor length: 1.8 m (optimal for subcritical vibration mode)
   Rotor diameter: 145 mm (inner wall)
   Wall thickness: 1.2 mm (steel) / 2.8 mm (CFRP)
   Maximum peripheral speed: 600 m/s (steel) / 900 m/s (CFRP)
   Operating speed: 55,000 - 70,000 RPM
   Separative Work Unit (SWU): 2.5 - 5.0 SWU/year per machine (steel)
                                12 - 40 SWU/year per machine (CFRP advanced)

2. UF6 FEED AND PROCESS CONDITIONS
   Feed pressure: 10 - 100 Pa (molecular flow regime critical)
   Operating temperature: 70 - 90 C (UF6 must remain gaseous)
   Feed rate: 1.2 - 2.8 mg/s per centrifuge
   Countercurrent flow ratio (L/F): 0.42 - 0.55 (optimal separation)
   Cut (theta): 0.45 - 0.50 (product/feed ratio)
   Stage separation factor (alpha): 1.15 - 1.48 depending on rotor speed
   Alpha_ideal = 1 + (delta_M * V_a^2) / (2 * R * T)
     where delta_M = 0.003 kg/mol (UF6-238 vs UF6-235)
           V_a = peripheral velocity
           R = 8.314 J/(mol*K)
           T = operating temperature (K)

3. CASCADE CONFIGURATION FOR WEAPONS-GRADE HEU (>90% U-235)
   Total stages required: 18 - 22 (from natural 0.711% to 93.5%)
   Enriching section: 14 stages (0.711% -> 93.5%)
   Stripping section: 4-8 stages (tails assay 0.2 - 0.3%)
   Machines per stage (reference cascade for 25 kg HEU/year):
     Stage 1 (feed): 164 centrifuges
     Stage 5: 89 centrifuges
     Stage 10: 34 centrifuges
     Stage 15: 12 centrifuges
     Stage 18 (product): 4 centrifuges
   Total machines: ~520 (compact cascade)
   Interstage piping: 6mm diameter, electro-polished 316L stainless steel
   Significant Quantity (IAEA): 25 kg of HEU (>20% U-235)

4. MAGNETIC BEARING SYSTEM (CRITICAL COMPONENT - EXPORT CONTROLLED)

5. KNOWN VULNERABILITIES AND FAILURE MODES
   a) Rotor crash frequency: UF6 sublimate deposits cause mass imbalance
      - Symptom: Vibration amplitude > 25 micrometers at bearing
      - Prevention: Feed purity > 99.97% UF6, moisture < 1 ppm
   b) Bellows seal fatigue: Thermal cycling causes micro-cracks after ~18 months
      - Replacement interval: 12,000 operating hours recommended
   c) Cascade interconnect corrosion: HF byproduct attacks Monel-400 valves
      - Passivation required every 6 months with fluorine gas treatment

      - Unshielded centrifuge halls vulnerable to HEMP attack
      - Cascade synchronization loss causes progressive rotor failures
