NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (Standard Template v3.2)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
[DATE] by and between [COMPANY NAME] ("Disclosing Party") and
[RECIPIENT] ("Receiving Party").

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION shall mean all technical, business,
   financial, and operational information disclosed by either party.

2. OBLIGATIONS: Receiving Party shall hold Confidential Information
   in strict confidence for a period of five (5) years.

3. EXCLUSIONS: Information that (a) is publicly available, (b) was
   known prior to disclosure, (c) is independently developed.

4. REMEDIES: Breach may result in irreparable harm. Disclosing Party
   is entitled to seek injunctive relief without posting bond.
