Alpaca - Instruction-Following Dataset

Source: GPT-3.5 auto-generated + human curation (Stanford, 2023)

Size: ~52,000 instruction-response pairs

Language: English

Format: JSON, each entry contains instruction, input (optional), and output fields

License: Apache 2.0 (code) / CC BY-NC 4.0 (data)

Distribution

  • Q&A instructions: ~35%
  • Creative writing: ~20%
  • Reasoning & logic: ~15%
  • Programming & code: ~10%
  • Other (translation, summarization, classification, etc.): ~20%

Quality Assessment

  • Strengths: Broad coverage, diverse instruction types
  • Weaknesses: GPT-3.5 generated, contains factual errors and inconsistent quality, some responses are overly brief
  • Applicable for: Basic instruction fine-tuning, research purposes

Sample

{
  "instruction": "Give three tips for staying healthy.",
  "input": "",
  "output": "1. Eat a balanced diet with plenty of fruits and vegetables.\n2. Exercise regularly, at least 30 minutes a day.\n3. Get enough sleep, aiming for 7-9 hours per night."
}
