DailyDialog - Multi-Turn Dialogue Dataset

Source: Public web crawl (Li et al., 2017)

Size: ~13,118 multi-turn daily conversations

Language: English

Format: Each dialogue contains multiple turns separated by "__eou__"

License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Annotations

  • Emotion labels: 7 classes (neutral, happy, surprise, sad, angry, disgust, fear)
  • Dialogue act labels: 4 classes (inform, question, directive, commissive)
  • Topic labels: 10 classes (ordinary life, school, work, travel, health, etc.)

Quality Assessment

  • Strengths: Covers everyday life scenarios, well-structured multi-turn dialogues
  • Weaknesses: Inconsistent quality, some dialogues lack semantic coherence, noticeable noise
  • Applicable for: Basic dialogue model pre-training, conversational sentiment analysis

Sample

User: How was your weekend?
Assistant: It was great! I went hiking with my friends.
User: That sounds fun! Where did you go?
Assistant: We went to the national park. The weather was perfect.
